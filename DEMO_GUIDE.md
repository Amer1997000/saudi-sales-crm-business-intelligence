# Recruiter Demonstration Guide

## Five-minute route

1. Open **Executive Overview** and read the synthetic-data and browser-local storage disclosure.
2. Use **Getting Started · Five-Minute Walkthrough** to jump directly to interactive modules.
3. In **Lead Management**, qualify a synthetic lead and review conversion controls. Avoid entering any real customer data.
4. In **Sales Pipeline**, inspect Kanban/table views and stage history. In **Quotations**, preview the controlled 15% demonstration VAT calculation.
5. Finish in **Revenue Forecasting** and **Sales Analytics**, where every figure derives from the stored records. Reset the demo when finished.

No login, installation or special configuration is required on the future static portfolio site. All eight navigation modules are functional.

## Phase 3 walkthrough

1. Open **Sales Pipeline**, switch between Kanban and table views, and filter by stage, account or representative.
2. Move `Head office workspace refresh` between Qualification and Discovery; open its History to see each transition.
3. Try moving that deal to Quotation Submitted. The system should explain that an Issued or Accepted quotation is required rather than bypassing the gate.
4. Move an eligible synthetic opportunity to Closed Lost and provide a reason. Confirm it disappears from open pipeline totals, then reset the demo.
5. Open **Follow-up Planner**. Review overdue and upcoming views, complete an overdue task, and confirm the dashboard overdue indicator changes.
6. Create a synthetic activity linked to a lead, account or opportunity. Review the follow-up-gap indicators; the application must not create follow-ups automatically.

## Phase 4 walkthrough

1. Open **Quotations** and inspect an existing quotation's professional preview. Use the print button to open the browser print workflow.
2. Build a synthetic Draft quotation for an opportunity. Add line items and confirm subtotal, discount, taxable amount, 15% VAT and final total update automatically.
3. Save and issue the quotation. For a new workflow, move its opportunity to Quotation Submitted, accept the quotation, then move the opportunity to Closed Won.
4. Confirm an Issued quotation cannot be edited and invalid status paths are unavailable.
5. Open **Revenue Forecasting**. Filter by period, stage, Saudi region, category and representative; compare each row's value × probability with the weighted total.
6. Confirm Closed Won records appear only in Closed Won commercial value, not open or weighted pipeline.
7. Refresh the browser to confirm persistence, then use the two-step reset to restore synthetic demonstration records.

## Phase 5 walkthrough

1. Open **Executive Overview**. Expand KPI definitions and review the sales funnel, pipeline mix, follow-up performance, quotation summary and record-derived management observations.
2. Confirm the page labels pipeline estimates and Closed Won commercial value separately from recognized accounting revenue.
3. Open **Sales Analytics**. Review each KPI's numerator, denominator and date basis.
4. Filter by Western region or Fahad Al-Qahtani. Confirm KPI cohorts and all charts update consistently.
5. Select an industry or product category and inspect pipeline, regional, representative and monthly trend reconciliation.
6. Choose a date range with no eligible leads or closed outcomes. Confirm affected metrics display Unavailable rather than misleading 0%.
7. Change a lead, opportunity, quotation or activity in its operational module, return to analytics, and confirm recalculation from stored records.
8. Refresh to verify persistence and use the two-step reset to restore the original synthetic dataset.

Dataset as-of: 16 November 2026. Five fictional accounts cover Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Madinah and Abha; extra leads cover Khobar, Tabuk and Riyadh. Email addresses use reserved `.invalid` domains. No real people, customers or measured outcomes are represented.

Do not enter confidential data. Browser storage lacks production encryption, backup, access control and multi-user support.
